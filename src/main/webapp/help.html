Help & User Guide

🚀 Getting Started

  1. Login: Use your assigned username and password to access the system
  2. Dashboard: After login, you'll see the main dashboard with quick access to all features
  3. Navigation: Use the sidebar menu to navigate between different sections

💡 Tip: Always remember to log out after finishing your work, especially on shared computers.

📝 Registering New Appointments

  1. Click on "New Appointment" in the sidebar menu
  2. The Appointment Number is auto-generated - don't modify it
  3. Enter the Patient's Full Name
  4. Enter the complete Address
  5. Enter a valid 10-digit Contact Number
  6. Enter Email (optional but recommended)
  7. Select the Dentist from dropdown
  8. Choose the Treatment Type
  9. Select Appointment Date (cannot be in the past)
  10. Select Appointment Time
  11. Add any Notes (optional)
  12. Click "Register Appointment"

⚠️ Note: All fields marked with * are required. Double-check patient information before submitting.

🔍 Searching for Appointments

  1. Go to "Search Patient" section
  2. Enter the Appointment Number
  3. Click "Search" button
  4. View complete appointment details in the results

💡 Tip: Appointment numbers follow the format: APT-YYYY-XXX (e.g., APT-2024-001)

💰 Generating Bills

  1. Go to "Billing" section
  2. Enter the Appointment Number
  3. Click "Generate Bill"
  4. Review the bill details carefully
  5. Click "Print Bill" to print the receipt

💡 Tip: Bills include consultation fee (Rs. 500), treatment cost, and 8% tax.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What if I forget my password?

A: Contact the system administrator to reset your password. For security reasons, passwords cannot be reset by users themselves.

Q: Can I cancel an appointment?

A: Yes, search for the appointment and update its status to "Cancelled". Contact the patient to inform them.

Q: How do I handle emergency appointments?

A: Register the appointment as normal and add a note indicating it's an emergency. Inform the dentist immediately.

Q: What should I do if the system is slow?

A: Close unnecessary browser tabs, clear browser cache, or contact IT support if the issue persists.

⚠️ Common Issues & Solutions

  • Login Failed: Ensure caps lock is off and try again. Contact admin if issue persists.
  • Duplicate Appointment Number: Refresh the page to generate a new number.
  • Data Not Found: Verify the appointment number is correct and try again.
  • Print Not Working: Check printer connection and browser print settings.
